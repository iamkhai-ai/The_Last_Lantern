Scenario: docs/scenario-v2/08-ruins-of-two-halves.md (the v2 draft). Gameplay and layout are FIXED. Redraw the look only.

Story frame: a moonlit temple courtyard, quiet and ruined. A stone statue with four notches stands in the centre. Two mirrors sit south-west and south-east. A Sun lock is on each side and a Moon lock is in the middle. A practice column and a test moon sit in the south-west forecourt. A seedling bed with a stone curtain sits north-west of the statue. A sanctuary door sits in the north wall. A beacon stone with a worn inscription sits in the north wall. The Shadow is a guardian figure who stands in the light.

Palette: the garden art of Level 07 is the reference family (cool slate-grey stone, worn brass, dark timber, moss in joints, muted, moonlit). Sun elements are warm brass. Moon elements are cool blue-violet. Keep the base dark and neutral so beams and light read at runtime.

HARD RULES (every asset)
1. Pixel art, nearest-neighbour, no anti-aliasing, no dithering. Transparent background for every sprite. Each family uses at most 128 colours.
2. Keep the exact canvas size and bottom-centre pivot below. Do not change the silhouette or orientation of any state. Each state must read the same way as the current file of the same name, only restyled.
3. Do NOT bake any light, beam, glow, lamp, lock glyph, shadow, text, character or UI. The runtime draws all of these.
4. Do NOT regenerate shadow-notch-0..3.png. They are measured overlays tied to the statue geometry. Keep the current four files unchanged.
5. Do NOT change the shared Shadow sprites under assets/levels/shared/characters/the-shadow/. They are also used by Levels 07, 09, the ending and the observatory. The Shadow is a separate decision (see section D).
